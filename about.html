Sector 1 — the driver

Coffee, cars, computers.

I build data-heavy web applications and lead the people who build them. Outside of that I mess with whatever has my attention: animation, hardware, race telemetry. Most of it ends up on this site.

I started in motorsport electronics, which is where the telemetry habit comes from. Every system should tell you what it’s doing, and every number on the dash should mean something. I still think about software that way.
