One lap,
one voice.

The rules that keep the home page (a live three.js lap) and every detail page (plain, dark, focused) feeling like the same site. Everything on this page is rendered from site.css, so if it looks right here it is right everywhere.

Principles Color Type Space & layout Voice Components Page anatomy Motion Assets & licences
01

Principles.

Content drives

The lap is a backdrop, not the subject. Text always sits on a surface that guarantees contrast; the scene is what shows through the gaps. Detail pages drop the scene entirely.

Telemetry voice

Labels are mono, tracked and small — like a data logger. Headlines are condensed and loud. Body is calm. Never all three at the same volume.

One accent

Amber is for the current thing: the active link, the eyebrow, the primary button, the dot on the map. Green and red exist only as telemetry (throttle/brake, go/stop) and never decorate.

One kind of thing

There is no separate blog. Every entry is a project: an app, a toy, or just the write-up. A few are featured as cards; all of them appear in the log and share one detail template. The badge says what you can do with it — PLAY, READ, or WATCH.

Racing as metaphor, lightly

Sectors, pit wall, finish line — used as section labels and never explained. If a racing term needs a footnote, cut it.

Real over invented

No fake stats, no lorem ipsum, no placeholder projects presented as real. Unknown facts stay as visible [BRACKETS] until filled.

04

Space & layout.

--s-1 … --s-9 · 4 8 12 16 24 32 48 64 96
Columns

--col 1040px for sections and grids; --col-narrow 720px for reading. Gutter 48px desktop, 20px phone.

Sections

64px vertical padding on the home page, 48px on detail pages, a 1px --line between sections. Every section opens with eyebrow + h2 (.section-head). Detail pages also step the display sizes down (h1 ≤ 88px, h2 ≤ 52px) — they are dense on purpose; the home page is where space is spent.

Corners

None. Square edges everywhere — buttons, cards, images, tags. The only curves on the site are the track.

Borders over shadows

Surfaces are separated by 1px hairlines and a slightly lighter fill, never drop shadows.

05

Voice.

Headlines

Short, declarative, a little dry. “Built on my own time.” “Where I’ve raced.” Full stops on headlines are fine; exclamation marks are not.

Eyebrows

Position + subject: SECTOR 2 — PERSONAL PROJECTS, PROJECT 001 — BUILD · NOV 2021, PIT WALL — ABOUT & RÉSUMÉ. Em dash, all caps, no trailing punctuation.

Body

First person, plain, concrete. Say what it is, why it exists, what you learned. One joke per page, maximum.

Calls to action

Verb first, arrow last: “Read the story →”, “Open the terminal →”. Never “Click here”.

The two shells share the nav, footer, tokens and every component. A visitor should be able to tell they are on the same site with the scene switched off.

08

Motion.

Scroll is throttle

On the home page scrolling in either direction drives the car forward. Position follows with a critically damped spring (0.55s). Nothing else is scroll-linked.

Eased navigation

In-page links ride to their target (0.5–2.4s by distance, ease-in-out). A wheel or key from the visitor cancels the ride.

Head stays still

The camera aims through the windscreen in the car’s own frame; corner glance ≈ 6°, bob 3mm, roll under 1°. No parallax on content.

Detail pages

No animation beyond hover colour (150ms). Motion is what the home page is for.

Reduced motion

prefers-reduced-motion: direct 1:1 tracking, no bob, no roll, no transitions.

Assets & licences.

Track

“Drift Race Track Free” by Nicholas-3D — CC BY-ND 4.0. Used unmodified (render flags only); the traced driving line is our own file. No re-encoding or stripping.

Never

Images

Project images live in assets/images/<slug>/<slug>-N.jpg; models in assets/models/; -0 is the card thumbnail, 16:10.
